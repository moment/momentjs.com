---
title: Legacy Integrations
---

These integrations and tools are retained for people maintaining existing
applications. They are legacy, inactive, or have an unclear maintenance status
and are not recommended for new projects. Their instructions may be outdated or
no longer work with current versions of the surrounding ecosystem.

⚠️ **Warning:** These integrations are maintained for Moment 2.x only and will
likely be removed in a future major release.
